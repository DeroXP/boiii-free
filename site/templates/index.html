Mod stacking

Your whole Workshop, in one game.

Turn your mods on, put them in order, launch. Xerces loads them together and checks which mods and maps fit before you launch, not after the game falls over.

How stacking works

Your load order.

The top mod is the main mod; where two mods ship the same file, the higher one wins. Every mod's entry script still runs, so mod menus work side by side.

Nothing left out.

The game has room for eight of the channels maps and mods use for lights, sounds and on-screen text. Big stacks need more, so Xerces shares one and every mod keeps its effects.

Friends join the same stack.

Players who join get the host's mods in the host's order, and are told which ones they are missing, with the Workshop page for each.

Checked with an eight-mod stack on every installed map
When something breaks

It tells you what happened.

A crash or a freeze leaves a report you can read: what the game was doing, your mods in load order, and the script that was running. The next launch offers safe mode.

When the game crashes

Safe mode.

After a crash, Home offers the next launch with mods off, so you are back in the game while you sort the stack out.

Find the culprit.

Xerces can launch halves of the crashed stack in turn and narrow it down to the mod, or the setting, that caused it.

Your reports, your call.

Crash reports help fix things and are on by default. Settings > Privacy turns them off and deletes the ones already sent.

Modding support

Power to mod authors.

Workshop mods work as they are. A mod that wants more says so in a small manifest: what it adds, what it needs, and what it may do. GSC scripts get hooks, console commands, and file and data helpers.

GSC scripting additions 
my.mod/xerces.mod.json{
  "schema": 1,
  "id": "my.mod",
  "name": "My Mod",
  "version": "0.1.0",
  "requires": { "modes": ["zm"] },
  "scripts": {
    "entry": ["scripts/zm/my_mod.gsc"]
  },
  "capabilities": {
    "gsc": { "replacefunc": false },
    "native_code": false
  }
}
// xerces_cli mod new my.mod   makes one
// xerces_cli mod lint my.mod  checks it
Enhanced client

The game you know, smoother.

Built on BOIII, with loads that keep up with your PC, a sharper picture, and Zombies options for the way you play. On by default where they have been played through; a switch away where they have not.

What is in it

Faster loads.

The loading screen keeps up with your PC, solo matches skip the lobby's waits, and the game starts about six seconds sooner. Town Reimagined with three mods: 9 s, was 34 s.

A sharper picture.

Render below your screen's resolution and let AMD FSR 1 bring it back up with the edges kept. NVIDIA DLSS and DLAA with NVIDIA's own file.

Zombies, your way.

XERCES SETTINGS in the lobby: start round, points, self-revives, round skips. CHARACTERS: every character mod's characters on one screen, with a live view.
